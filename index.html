A little about me

I'm Nakshatra. I build voice AI and fullstack products, and I read too much philosophy for someone who writes this much code.

  • Final-year engineering student at VIT (Vellore Institute of Technology), from Pune.
  • Spent the last few years building fullstack products. Now deep into voice AI: latency, interruptions, and why a 300ms pause can make an agent feel broken.
  • Philosophy and psychology got to me before code did. They're why I ask “why should we build this?” before “how?”
  • Looking to join early-stage startups where I can own a problem from idea to production.
